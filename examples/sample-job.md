Description:
About Saltmere
Saltmere builds payment and checkout tools for independent online merchants. More than 25,000 shops use us to take card payments, offer digital wallets and bank transfers, and get paid faster. We are about 180 people, working remotely across the US.

About the role
We are hiring a Senior Product Manager to own Checkout: the hosted checkout page, the embeddable payment form and the experiments that decide how many shoppers finish paying. You will set the product roadmap, work with engineering, design and data science, and be accountable for checkout conversion and payment success across our merchants. You will report to the Director of Product, Payments.

What you'll do
- Own the checkout roadmap and write PRDs that engineers and designers can build from.
- Run experiments (A/B tests) on checkout flows, payment methods and error messages, and decide what ships from the data.
- Improve conversion and authorization rate by finding where shoppers drop off in the funnel and fixing it with your team.
- Add payment methods, such as digital wallets, bank transfers and buy now, pay later, working with our payments and partnerships teams.
- Spend time with merchants every week and turn what you learn into priorities.
- Work with risk and support to reduce fraud and failed payments without adding friction for good shoppers.
- Launch features with sales, marketing and customer success: write the launch plan, the merchant-facing docs and the enablement.
- Report on KPIs and results to company leadership each quarter.

What we're looking for
- 5+ years of product management experience, ideally in checkout, payments, ecommerce or fintech.
- A record of improving conversion through experiments, with results you can put a number on.
- Strong SQL. You pull your own data and are comfortable with analytics tools.
- Clear writing, and the ability to explain trade-offs to engineers and executives.
- Experience setting a roadmap across several cross-functional teams with competing priorities.

Nice to have
- Experience with fraud, risk or PCI compliance.
- Experience with international payments and multiple currencies.
- Experience with subscription billing.

Pay and benefits
Base salary $165,000 – $205,000, depending on experience and location, plus a bonus and equity. Health, dental and vision coverage, a home-office budget, and 4 weeks of paid time off.

Saltmere is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applicants of every background.
