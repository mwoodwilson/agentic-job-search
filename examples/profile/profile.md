## Senior Product Manager, Larkfield Commerce (March 2023 – Present)

**Location:** Chicago, IL. Larkfield Commerce is a checkout and payments platform for independent online stores.

What I did:
- I own Checkout: the hosted checkout page, the embeddable payment form and the wallets. Team of 5 engineers and 1 designer, plus a data scientist shared with two other teams. I report to the Director of Product, Payments.
- Replaced the old three-step checkout with a single page. Used SQL to find that step 2 (shipping and payment) lost the most shoppers, wrote the PRD, designed it with my designer, and rolled it out in stages over 6 weeks, finishing in August 2024.
- Run the weekly experiment review with data science and engineering. The backlog is ranked by expected lift and I decide what ships.
- Added Apple Pay and other digital wallets to the hosted checkout with payments engineering (launched April 2024). Wrote the launch plan and ran the training for support and sales.
- Worked with payments engineering on smart retries for soft-declined cards (started Q1 2025). I wrote the rules for when to retry and what the shopper sees.
- Wrote the PRD for the embeddable payment form API (hosted fields) with developer relations. Launched in Q2 2025.
- Talk to merchants every week and run a merchant advisory group of 12 stores that meets monthly.
- Present the checkout KPIs (conversion, authorization rate, payment success rate) to company leadership every quarter.

### Confirmed facts: Senior Product Manager (exact numbers and details to use as written)
- 38,000 active merchants use the hosted checkout.
- One-page checkout: checkout completion rose from 61.0% to 66.8% (+5.8 points) within 3 months of the full rollout in August 2024. Finance estimated +$31M in annualized merchant sales.
- Experiments: 46 A/B tests run in 2025 and 19 shipped as winners. Mobile checkout conversion was up 12% YoY (Q4 2025 vs. Q4 2024).
- Wallets: Apple Pay and other digital wallets launched in April 2024. Wallets were 27% of mobile orders within 2 months of launch. I wrote the launch plan and the enablement, and trained 40 support and sales reps.
- Smart retries: authorization rate rose from 88.4% to 90.9% (+2.5 points) since Q1 2025. Finance estimated $4.2M in annual merchant sales recovered.
- Embeddable payment form API: launched in Q2 2025. 1,900 merchants integrated since Q2 2025. Median integration time was 3 days, down from 11 days on the old API.
- Merchant research: 6 merchant calls per week. The calls and the advisory group drove 7 of the 12 roadmap items shipped in 2025.
- Roadmap: I set the checkout roadmap for 5 engineers and 1 designer. I present the quarterly KPI review to company leadership.

## Product Manager, Wrenfield Health (June 2020 – February 2023)

**Location:** Chicago, IL. Wrenfield Health makes a patient app for booking appointments and paying bills at outpatient clinics (120 clinics).

What I did:
- Owned booking and bill pay in the patient web and mobile apps. Squad of 6 engineers and 1 designer. The roadmap spanned 3 teams: scheduling, billing and notifications.
- Used SQL and Looker to analyze the booking funnel. Found that 41% of patients quit at the insurance step, and redesigned it with an insurance card scan and a "skip for now" option (launched March 2021).
- Launched text reminders for appointments in September 2021.
- Launched online bill pay for all 120 clinics in 2022.
- Interviewed patients and clinic staff, and wrote the PRDs for the 2022 roadmap.

### Confirmed facts: Product Manager (exact numbers and details to use as written)
- Booking funnel: 41% of patients quit booking at the insurance step. After the insurance card scan redesign (March 2021), drop-off at that step fell from 41% to 27% within 3 months. Online bookings grew 35% YoY in 2021.
- Text reminders: no-show rate fell from 18% to 11% within 6 months of launch (September 2021), across 120 clinics.
- Online bill pay: launched in 2022. Patients paying their balance online rose from 12% to 38% within 4 months. Clinics processed $14M through it in 2022.
- Research and PRDs: 60 patient and clinic-staff interviews in 2021. 9 PRDs written for the 2022 roadmap.
